Before you post

  1. Save the screenshot of the call, SMS or chat first.
  2. Note the exact phone number, UPI ID or link used.
  3. If money was lost, call 1930 within 24 hours for the best chance of recovery.
  4. Never share your OTP, PIN or password with anyone — including someone claiming to be your bank.

Helplines

Who to contact

These are official Government of India helplines. Save them in your phone contacts today.

1930

National Cyber Crime Helpline

24×7, run by I4C, Ministry of Home Affairs — for financial fraud and cybercrime.

112

National Police Helpline

For any emergency requiring immediate police assistance.

181

Women Helpline

24×7 support for women facing harassment, including online abuse.

cybercrime.gov.in

National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

File a detailed, trackable complaint online, in multiple languages.
